What counts as holding

Your lowest balance of the last 24 hours counts (2 hours in the first 48 hours after launch). Tokens sent to you, not bought, count after 7 days. Only regular wallets count.

All holders share one daily pool. When your allowance or the pool runs out, your key keeps working on low-cost models until 00:00 UTC.
