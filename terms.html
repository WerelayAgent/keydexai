The service

Through one API key, Keydex AI gives your agent AI models and tools for stock tokens on Solana. It is provided as is, without warranties, and parts of it depend on outside services.

Trial keys

A trial key draws from a free daily allowance for each visitor, expires after 24 hours and works with low-cost models only, with replies up to 512 tokens. Each visitor can get up to 3 keys in 24 hours.

Your keys and your agent

Keep your API keys and connector URLs private. A wallet can have up to 10 active keys, and all of them draw from the wallet’s daily allowance.

What your agent signs

Every transaction is returned unsigned. Your agent signs and sends it, and you are responsible for what it signs. Onchain transactions can’t be reversed.

Daily allowance

Once the Keydex AI token ($KEYDEX) launches, holding it gives the wallet you sign in with a daily allowance for model calls, in tiers by the value held, in dollars at the median pool price of the last 7 days. Until then, wallet keys use the same free daily allowance as a trial key. All holders share a daily pool. Only the lowest balance of the last 24 hours counts (2 hours in the first 48 hours after launch), tokens received by plain transfer count after 7 days, and only regular wallets count. When the allowance or the pool runs out, your key works under the free trial rules until 00:00 UTC. We set the tiers and can change or pause them. The allowance does not carry over and can’t be transferred. The token is not an investment, and Keydex AI promises nothing about its price.

Stock tokens

Stock tokens are not available to US persons and are restricted in Canada, the UK, Switzerland and other countries. This service shows data and builds transactions. It does not sell stock tokens. Nothing on Keydex AI is investment advice.

Outside services

This service relies on Solana nodes, a stock data service, LI.FI for trade routes, Relay for funding and model providers reached through a routing service. Their availability, prices and fees are outside our control.

Acceptable use

Use Keydex AI only where the law allows it, stock token restrictions included. Don’t collect trial keys in bulk or get around the trial limit, resell access, overload or attack the service, or use it to break another service’s terms.

Suspension
