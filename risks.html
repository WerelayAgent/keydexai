Before you trade

Risks

What can go wrong when your agent trades stock tokens through keyfo.

Last updated November 9, 2026

TermsPrivacyRisks
TermsPrivacyRisks

On this page

Pool prices are not exchange prices

Prices on Keydex AI come from each stock token’s pool on Solana. They can differ from the stock’s exchange price and can move sharply within a day.

Thin pools: large trades move the price

Some pools hold little money. A large trade moves the price, and you can get less than the quote. Pools under $50K are marked Thin pool.

Routes come from LI.FI and Relay

build_trade gets its route from LI.FI, fund_agent from Relay. A route can be unavailable, expire or change before signing, or fail onchain. Their fees are their own.

Morpho vaults

Through morpho_rates and build_morpho_deposit, your agent can read and use Morpho vaults on Solana. Vaults carry smart contract, curator and market risk, and a deposit can lose value. Rates change, withdrawals depend on the vault’s liquidity, and a loan against a stock token is liquidated if its price falls enough.

What your agent signs is final

Transactions are returned unsigned. What your agent signs and sends is final onchain and is your responsibility. Slippage is 1% by default and can be set from 0.1% to 10%.

Models can be wrong

Model requests pass through a model routing service to the provider of the model you pick. Answers can be wrong, numbers included. Check them against the tool results before your agent acts.

A connector URL carries your key

A URL with ?key= in it works like the key, and anyone who has it can spend your allowance with it. Revoke the key in the app if the URL leaks.

Outside services Keydex AI depends on

Solana nodes, a stock data service, LI.FI, Relay and model providers all have to answer. When one of them is down, that part of Keydex AI stops working.

Stock tokens and your country

Stock tokens are not available to US persons and are restricted in Canada, the UK, Switzerland and other countries. This service shows data and builds transactions. It does not sell stock tokens. Check what applies where you live.

The token and its allowance

We set the holder allowance. Its tiers and the shared daily pool can change, and on a busy day the pool can run out before 00:00 UTC. The token is not an investment, and nothing on Keydex AI promises its price.

Not investment advice

Quotes, portfolio data and model answers are information. Nothing on Keydex AI is investment, legal or tax advice.
