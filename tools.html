Tools

Each tool your agent can call through Keydex AI: what it does and where its data comes from. Read tools run here without a key.

Stock tokens

stock_quote

Price, 24h change and pool depth for one stock token or up to 20 at once.

Pool prices from the stock token pools on Solana, cached for 15 s.Pool prices from the stock token pools on Solana, cached for 15 s. Bid and ask from Solana’s API, cached for 15 s.

Raw result

stock_portfolio
Every stock token and ETF in a wallet, valued in dollars, plus its SOL.
Balances read from Solana in one call, priced from the pools. Cached for 10 s per wallet.Balances read from Solana in one call, priced from the pools, or from Solana’s API when a token has no pool. Dividends and splits from Solana’s API, cached for 1 h.
Raw result
stock_moversNew in 1.6
Today’s biggest movers among stock tokens.
Ranked by the pool’s 24h change, from the stock token pools, cached for 15 s.Ranked by the pool’s 24h change, or by dollars minted and burned today and the place in the day’s range, from Solana’s API. Cached for 15 s.
Raw result
stock_eventsNew in 1.6
Upcoming dividends and splits for stock tokens.
Dividends and splits from Solana’s API, cached for 1 h.
Raw result
Trades
build_trade
Builds an unsigned buy or sale of a stock token for SOL. Your agent signs it before the quote expires.
Routes and quotes from LI.FI, tested on the network before the reply. A quote is cached for 30 s.
Needs a keyConnect
Funding
fund_agent
Builds a transfer from any of 15 networks to your agent’s wallet, arriving as SOL. The payer signs it.
Routes and quotes from Relay.

Read from your key when you ask. Without a key: the free read calls left this hour.

Rates of Morpho USDG vaults and of loans against stock tokens.
Morpho’s public API, cached for 2 min. Rates change; the numbers are a snapshot.

Morpho rates, cached for 2 min.

Calls for a Morpho vault, tested on the network before the reply. Your agent signs them.

Models
list_modelsNew in 1.8
Lists the models your key can call, with prices and what each supports.
Read from Keydex AI’s cached model catalog. Free, and it calls no model.

Token approvals read from Solana in batches, against the spenders Keydex AI knows. Cached for 30 s per wallet.
