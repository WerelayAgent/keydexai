        Common questions

        Who holds the private keys?

        Your agent does. Transactions come back unsigned, and your agent signs them itself. No Keydex AI API or MCP tool asks for a private key.

        Do I need to buy credits?

        No. There are no credits or top-ups. Tools are free, and model calls run on a daily allowance that resets at 00:00 UTC.

        Do I need a wallet?

        No. A trial key works without one. Sign in with a wallet to create, name and revoke your own keys; until the token launches, they get the same free daily allowance.

        What do you log?

        Prompts and replies are not stored. For each call we log the time, key, model or tool, tokens, cost and status, as you see in Usage. Our server log also keeps IP addresses and request paths.

        Is Keydex AI built on Solana?

        Yes. Keydex AI is built independently on Solana Mainnet.

        Can anyone use stock tokens?

        No. Stock tokens are not available to US persons and are restricted in Canada, the UK, Switzerland and other countries. This service shows data and builds transactions. It does not sell stock tokens.
